Hello, I'm Yufei! I'm an MS student in Computer Science at the [University of Washington](https://www.cs.washington.edu/), where I also completed my BS in 2025.

I'm incredibly fortunate to work with [Scott Geng](https://www.scottgeng.com/) and [Prof. Pang Wei Koh](https://koh.pw/) in the UW NLP Group on language models and generation diversity. Check out our recent work, [Gacha Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01382)!

In summer 2026, I was a Machine Learning Engineer Intern at [LinkedIn Core AI](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yfzhang3), where I built a dynamic working-memory layer for AI agents and evaluated long-context memory systems. Previously, I worked on large-scale systems and infrastructure at [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yfzhang3) and [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/).

Alongside my studies, I'm the head teaching assistant for machine learning at UW. See [teaching](/teaching/) for more details.

Outside of academics, I love endurance sports, mostly running and cycling, and am obsessed with making (and eating!!) poke. Most importantly, I love spending time with my family and friends, who remind me how truly precious life is.
